Add Ushers to your phone

It then opens like any other app, works with no signal, and can alert you about duties, reminders and countersignatures. Alerts on an iPhone only work once this is done.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Ushers in Safari.
  2. Tap Share at the foot of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Close Safari and open Ushers from the Home Screen.
  5. Tap the bell and allow alerts.

Android

  1. Open Ushers in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install if it offers; otherwise tap the menu (three dots) and choose Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Ushers from the Home Screen.
  4. Tap the bell and allow alerts.

If something is in the way

No Share button: you are not in Safari. Open the same address in Safari and start again. No alerts after installing: open Ushers from the Home Screen, not from the browser, then tap the bell. Alerts blocked: allow notifications for Ushers in the phone's own settings.
