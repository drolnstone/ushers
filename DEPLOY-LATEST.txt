USHERS
======
  app v0.3.20 · server w0.3.19 · sheet v0.3.4

  Everything in the handover, built. Since app v0.3.12 · server w0.3.7 ·
  sheet v0.3.1:

  - An usher is told when they are taken off a duty, or the event is off.
  - Phones stay quiet between 21:00 and 08:00, except a countersign or
    approval request.
  - Signing in: the name used last is already chosen, the list shows before
    the server answers, the fourth digit signs in.
  - The screens say what to do and what happened, never how the app works.
  - A duty email carries a calendar entry for the usher's own diary, with a
    reminder the day before.
  - Every email ends with a link to the screen it is about.
  - "Check everything" on the sheet names people: alerts off, unreachable,
    still on the default PIN, not installed, Sundays short of ushers.
  - The Admin App shows what went out: how many phones and emails each
    message reached, including the ones it reached nobody with.
  - Both apps offer to add Ushers to the phone, and chase anybody who has
    not once a week. install.html is the one-page guide to share.
  - Home says who is in charge today, with a Call button.
  - An usher can say beforehand that they cannot make a duty; the Head Usher
    decides and names the cover.
  - The month's figures are emailed on the last Sunday evening, the year's
    in December. Money only to whoever may see it.
  - Repeating events (the prayer meeting, the retreat) as patterns in
    Settings, and extra reminders per kind of event.
  - Admin -> Message chooses who, when, and whether it stays on Home, and
    shows how many have read it.
