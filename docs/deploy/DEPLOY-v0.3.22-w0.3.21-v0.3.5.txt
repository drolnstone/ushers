USHERS
======
  app v0.3.22 · server w0.3.21 · sheet v0.3.5

  What the Driver App learnt between 10 and 17 October, taken for Ushers.

  1. Alerts, emails and refusals say facts only, no advice, and say "the
     Head Usher" where they said "the coordinator".
  2. Admin → Ushers takes only an 11-digit UK phone number.
  3. When an admin adds or removes someone, or changes their details, PIN
     or roles, that person and the other admins are told.
  4. Wrong PINs lock the phone they came from, not the name, so nobody can
     keep the Head Usher locked out.
  5. Turning alerts on says which step failed, and an old sign-up is
     made again.
  6. Admin → Ushers: tap a phone number to ring it.
  7. Every typing box shows a faded "e.g." sample.
  8. The sheet sets itself up after a new Code.gs, and emails you when the
     server stops (WATCH_EMAIL, or the sheet's owner).
  9. The browser journey runs on GitHub for every pull request.
 10. Deploy on merge is ready to switch on (DEPLOY.md, section 5).
